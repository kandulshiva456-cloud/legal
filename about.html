Who We Are

A Property-Focused Legal Practice.

Legal excellence, professional legal services, trusted documentation, expert consultation and seamless registration.

With over 30 years of experience, GO LEGALS is a trusted name in conveyancing and legal documentation, delivering precise, reliable and hassle-free service. Backed by decades of practice, the commitment stays on excellence, accuracy and professionalism.

The team works to ensure a seamless documentation process — examining property documents and government sanctions, preparing agreements and deeds, coordinating with parties and supporting registration at the Sub-Registrar Office. The aim is to make complex legal matters simpler and less stressful for the client.

  • Property legal focus — transactions, titles and registration
  • Documentation expertise across agreements and deeds
  • Due diligence, verification and document review
  • Practical guidance from first draft to final registration

What We Do

Documentation Work, Taken End to End.

Most property matters involve the same underlying work — verification, drafting, coordination and registration. GO LEGALS handles each of them.

  1. 01

    Verify

    Title investigation, scrutiny of property documents and examination of government sanctions and approvals.

  2. 02

    Draft

    Agreements for sale, sale deeds, powers of attorney, wills, gift, release, relinquishment and other documents.

  3. 03

    Register

    Documentation support and assistance at the Sub-Registrar Office, including weekend and holiday coordination where applicable.

  4. 04

    Follow Through

    Post-registration documentation assistance, including tax, electricity and Form 26QB formalities where applicable.

Payment

Flexible installment payment options may be available, depending on the chosen service package and scope of work. Pricing is discussed once the scope of your matter is clear.

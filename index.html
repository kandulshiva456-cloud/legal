About GO LEGALS

Experience That Makes Complex Documentation Simple.

Conveyancing is detail work. Titles, sanctions, agreements, deeds and registration formalities all have to align — and they have to be right the first time.

With over 30 years of experience, GO LEGALS is a trusted name in conveyancing and legal documentation — delivering precise, reliable and hassle-free service. Decades of practice shape every file: careful examination, clear drafting and steady coordination through to registration.

The work is practical rather than theoretical. Documents are read closely, government sanctions are examined, agreements and deeds are prepared with care, and each step is explained in plain language so that you always know where your matter stands.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Brief answers to the questions we are asked most often about scope and process.

What services does GO LEGALS provide?
GO LEGALS assists with property documentation, title verification, due diligence, legal drafting, registration assistance, SRO assistance and property-related tax and Form 26QB formalities, along with consultations and document review.
What is included in Complete Buyer Assist?
The Complete Buyer Assist Package includes a Title Investigation Report from experienced advocates, examination of property documents, examination of government sanctions, preparation of the Agreement for Sale, preparation of the Sale Deed and support at the Sub-Registrar Office.
Does GO LEGALS assist with registration?
Yes. Registration assistance includes preparation of the Agreement for Sale and Sale Deed, coordination for the appointment, and support at the Sub-Registrar Office. Schedules and procedures depend on the relevant authority and prevailing requirements.
Does GO LEGALS assist with agricultural land verification?
Yes. Agricultural land verification is a detailed legal check of the documents, title and applicable approvals connected to the land before purchase.
Does GO LEGALS assist with Form 26QB?
Yes. Form 26QB filing assistance is available for purchasers and sellers, including a dedicated service for transactions with more than two buyers or sellers, along with TAN assistance for the property purchaser.
Can I request a legal consultation?
Yes. You can request a Legal Consultation or a Physical Consultation with Lawyer. Consultations cover an in-person meeting, document review, strategy discussion and legal planning for the matter at hand.
What is included in document review?
Document review covers the review of any document connected to your matter — agreements, deeds, prior title papers or related paperwork — for correctness, compliance and risk. Confidential documents are shared only after your enquiry, through an agreed secure process.
